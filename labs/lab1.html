Step 4 — Rating distribution

  • Skewed positive: mode = 4.0, mean = 3.50
  • Whole stars used more than half-stars
  • Few 0.5–1.5 ratings
Selection bias: people rate what they chose to watch. 3.5 ≠ “average movie”.
The global mean (3.5) is already a baseline. Any model must beat it.

Step 5b — Users differ in how they rate

  • User means range from 1.3 to 5.0
  • Some users are generous, some harsh
User bias. A 4 from a harsh user means more than a 4 from a generous one.
